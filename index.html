FAQ

Questions carriers ask us

What is your dispatch fee?

4–7% of the gross of loads we book, depending on your situation: 6% for established carriers, 7% for new MC authorities, 5% for fleets of 2–4 trucks and 4% for fleets of 5+ trucks. No setup fee, no monthly minimum.

Do you dispatch reefer?

Yes — reefer is our primary niche. Temperature-controlled food and pharma freight pays more per mile, and we know reefer brokers, appointments and detention rules best.

Do I have to take every load you find?

No. SwiftLane works on no forced dispatch — every load is sent to you for approval first. If the rate or lane doesn't work for you, we keep searching.

How and when do I pay the dispatch fee?

The fee is a percentage of the gross of each completed load, billed weekly by invoice. You pay after the load is done — never in advance.

What documents do I need to start?

Your MC/DOT authority letter, W-9, certificate of insurance, and a signed dispatch agreement. If you're brand new, we'll walk you through each one.

Can you work with a brand-new MC authority?

Yes. New authorities have fewer broker options, and we focus on the brokers and load boards that accept new MCs while your authority ages and your options grow.

Do you help with factoring and invoicing?

Yes — we prepare and submit your invoices and PODs to the broker or your factoring company, and follow up until payment is released.
